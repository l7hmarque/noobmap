## 3. What will be done

- A **non-intrusive** scan to identify devices, exposed ports and services.
- A **report** with the risks found and remediation guidance.

## 4. What will NOT be done

- **No** active exploitation (no intrusion, no data capture).
- **No** automatic change of configuration on the network, router or firewall.
- **No** intentional disruption of the internet or connected devices.

## 5. Responsibilities

- The **client** declares to be the legal owner of the authorized network.
- The **provider** will operate only within the agreed network and time window.
- The recommended fixes will be applied **by the client** (or by the provider alongside the
  client), always after backing up the configuration.
